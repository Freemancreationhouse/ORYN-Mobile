ORYN Direct ESP32 V10.4.1 — Direct3.5 Persistent LAN Fix

Fixes:
- Direct mode no longer depends on the frontend active-table selection surviving reloads.
- Saved Direct calibration/host can keep the Direct transport active persistently.
- Home-WiFi discovery resolves fluidnc.local to a numeric LAN IPv4 when possible.
- Direct probe returns/stores the resolved numeric controller host.
- FluidNC G-code rejection no longer falsely marks the controller disconnected.
- Home marks controller online immediately after the Telnet transport connects; only transport-connect failure marks it offline.
- Background re-probe restores online state after a cold app start.
- Existing patterns, Pattern Designer, calibration math and THR conversion remain unchanged.
